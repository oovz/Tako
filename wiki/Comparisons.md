# Choosing Tako

[English](Comparisons) · [日本語](Comparisons.ja) · [简体中文](Comparisons.zh-CN) · [繁體中文](Comparisons.zh-TW) · [한국어](Comparisons.ko)

Tako is for readers who want to save manga while browsing in Chrome. Chapter selection and progress sit beside the page in the Side Panel.

## Does it fit how you read?

| You want to…                                       | How Tako helps                                                        |
| -------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Save several chapters without handling every image | Select chapters and add them to the queue                             |
| Read downloaded chapters in a comic reader         | Export CBZ files, with optional covers and ComicInfo.xml metadata     |
| Keep images in a familiar format                   | Choose ZIP archives or individual image files                         |
| Organize a local manga folder                      | Choose a save folder and set folder and filename templates            |
| Try unfinished chapters again                      | Retry failed chapters or restart a task from its recorded destination |
| See what happened to a download                    | Review save results and chapter history in Options                    |

## Check before installing

Tako requires Chrome 150 or newer and works with the [supported sites](Supported-Sites). Chapter availability follows those integrations and your access through each site’s website reader.

You select the chapters to download and keep Chrome running while they save. Tako's queue and history survive browser restarts, but interrupted work may need a retry.

The available formats are CBZ, ZIP, and individual images. Downloads begin with your chapter selection in Chrome.

## Try a chapter

Follow [Quick Start](Quick-Start) to download one chapter and open it in your preferred reader. If the result or save location is unclear, [Troubleshooting](Troubleshooting) explains the available actions.
